# MRJD Interpretation Diagnostics

## Design

Generated by `scripts/06_debug_mrjd_parameterization.py`. This was a controlled sensitivity run, not a paper-scale replication: 256 paths per estimate, 12 outer replications, jump rates `{0.01, 0.08, 0.70}`, and the subset of nominal ratio values `{0.20, 0.33, 0.50, 0.67, 1.00}`. It writes 12,420 method rows, a grouped summary, a variant register, and a diagnostic plot in `outputs/diagnostics/mrjd_parameterization/`.

## Conclusion

The diagnostic did not identify an interpretation that reproduces panel (d)'s approximate several-unit relative-difference range. The baseline 12-date put reaches 0.478059 in this 12-repetition run; the largest tested listed interpretation reaches 0.750143. The paper-image read near 5.8 is approximate and is not a machine-readable target, but the order-of-magnitude mismatch remains clear. The diagnostics do not establish which paper ambiguity explains the discrepancy, nor justify choosing a parameterization by visual resemblance.

Variant definitions and raw/grouped outputs are in `outputs/diagnostics/mrjd_parameterization/variant_register.csv`, `raw.csv`, and `summary.csv`. The associated figure is explicitly diagnostic only.
