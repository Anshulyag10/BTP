# Figure Specifications

## Figure 1 (paper p.747)

Four cases: GBM/Merton vanilla call (`16,384` paths); Vasicek/MRJD vanilla put (`256`); four-observation Asian call (`256`); twelve-observation Asian put by panel heading (`256`, caption says call). Upper plots median power for GradOne, GradTwo, CRN t-test by lambda. Lower plots relative difference in median option-price estimate. Pair 1 x axis is `lambda/sigma`; Pair 2 x-axis is `E|J|/sigma`. Figure caption says CRN tolerance `0.2%`.

## Figure 2 (paper p.748)

Same four cases, fixed ratio indicated in each panel; top: empirical rejection probability, bottom: median estimated power; path counts `2^6` to `2^14`; three lambdas and three methods. Caption describes median quantities.

## Figure 3 (Appendix A, paper p.750)

Same case/rate/ratiogrid as Figure 1; mean power and relative difference in mean price estimates. Caption sets CRN deviation to `0.2%` of diffusion price.

Exact data coordinates are not tabulated. Layout/style can be inspected from the supplied PDF; source curves can only be digitized approximately from low-resolution embedded plots.
